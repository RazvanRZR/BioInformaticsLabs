**Limitation:** These are FDC food-record searches, not evidence that each record reports all nine amino acids. Food-composition records often lack individual amino-acid values, and an absent value must be treated as **missing**, not zero. Low protein can help identify candidates, but it cannot establish the amount or ranking of any particular amino acid. Therefore, I can’t responsibly give numerical amounts or an exact lowest-food ranking without matching records that actually report the relevant amino acids for each food on the same 100-g edible basis.
